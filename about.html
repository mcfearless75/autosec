Who we are

360 Auto Security is an independent vehicle-security specialist based at Millards Industrial Estate in West Bromwich, serving Birmingham and the wider West Midlands.

We focus on one thing — fitting Thatcham-categorised security systems to a standard that holds up. That means manufacturer training, TASSA accreditation, clean wiring, hidden fitment, and a paper trail your insurer will actually accept.

Whether you’re protecting a brand-new Range Rover, a classic, a daily-driver or a fleet of vans, the install gets the same attention.

Autowatch Ghost II

The undetectable immobiliser.

The Ghost is a CAN bus immobiliser with no key fobs, no LED indicators and no radio signals. Even if a thief has your key, they can’t drive your car — they need to enter a personal PIN sequence first using buttons already in the vehicle.

  • Silent and weatherproof — no signal to detect or jam
  • PIN sequence via existing buttons (steering wheel / door / centre console)
  • Service Mode for valets, MOTs and dealerships
  • ECU replacement protection — new key won’t bypass it
